Parent Inquiries

Common Questions

My child already has heavy school homework. Will 1 hour add stress? +

On the contrary, it saves time! Distracted homework takes 2 to 3 hours because the mind wanders. Daily 10-minute Tratak and Pranayama doubles working concentration—allowing homework to finish in 45 minutes with greater accuracy.

Is this religious or scientific? +

VedIQ Academy is a strictly scientific + Vedic cognitive framework. We explain meditation through prefrontal cortex brainwave stabilization, Surya Namaskar through posture and oxygenation, and Vedic Maths as pattern arithmetic.

What if my child cannot sit still for even 10 minutes right now? +

We never force stillness. We begin Day 1 with dynamic, playful exercises like balloon breathing and drawing the Sun, progressing gradually to sustained focus over 30 days without tension.
